# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People evaluating or operating Android apps with the Anima accessibility agent, including hackathon judges demonstrating its capabilities on a device.

## Product Purpose

Anima maps an Android app autonomously and can replay requested GUI tasks through an on-device accessibility runtime. The app gives users a clear permission gate, a goal runner, and a dependable three-act demo.

## Positioning

It turns live Android UI exploration into a compact, stable App Knowledge Pack and replays learned skills without further LLM calls.

## Operating Context

The Android app is used on a phone during onboarding, live task execution, and in-person demonstrations. Accessibility and overlay permissions are explicit prerequisites for operating other apps; the demo deliberately works without them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app must retain its permission controls, goal runner, three-act demo, kill-switch language, and Material Android interactions. Network-free on-device operation and truthful consent language are required.

## Brand Commitments

The user requested a jet-black interface with buttons inspired by the supplied pearlescent, iridescent reference image.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, android/README.md, the Android layouts, and the user-supplied reference image. No commercial proof, customer data, or external imagery is assumed.

## Product Principles

- Make a powerful automation tool legible before it is operable.
- Keep consent, safety, and user control visible.
- Make the zero-LLM replay story easy to demonstrate.
- Preserve dependable native Android behavior.
